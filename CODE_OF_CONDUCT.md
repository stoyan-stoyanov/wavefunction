# Community expectations

Wavefunction welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be considerate, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Respect boundaries and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publishing another person’s private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, personal data, or private customer material in discussions or examples.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact. Contributors may ask for clarification or reconsideration without retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@morphologic.ai](mailto:contact@morphologic.ai), not in a public issue or discussion. Security vulnerabilities use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and project community spaces.
